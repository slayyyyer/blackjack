THE POINT

100,000 rounds is enough to measure the engine. It is not enough to measure a 0.4% edge.

Observed edge Exact edge 95% interval

A 95% confidence interval is a statement about the method, not a promise about this run. The interval is wide because one round can swing more than a full bet.

WHY THE MISS IS SMALL

The gap between the observed and exact edge is 0.185 percentage points. The standard error alone is 0.365 points. The simulation did not fail to find the edge; it found a sample whose center is very close and whose error bar is wider than the effect.

THE HOUSE EDGE IS AVERAGE, NOT A TREND

Blackjack does not lose a little on every round. The player wins 43,246 rounds, loses 47,547 and pushes 9,207. Naturals pay 3:2, and doubles and splits change the size of the bet. The 0.426% edge is the average of all those outcomes, not a line the bankroll follows.

What is exact

Dealer distributions and action EVs are exact for their composition. The 0.426% fresh-shoe edge is the composition-aware fixed-strategy solve; its split EV uses the documented independent-hand approximation.

The simulation deals from the real six-deck shoe with a 75% cut card. It settles splits, doubles, insurance and dealer peeks with the same rules the table uses.

What is approximated

Split EV uses the independent-hand approximation. The chart is total-dependent, not composition-dependent. The insurance grade treats the hole card as one unseen card rather than a peek.

The play harness matched the chart on 99.91% of decisions. The 105 exceptions are rare post-hit or legal-action states; their combined EV cost was 14.71 units over the run.

What this does not prove

One hundred thousand rounds cannot pin a sub-1% edge to three decimals. The 95% interval on the observed edge spans -0.47% to 0.96%, so this sample is consistent with break-even and with a house edge above 1%. The exact solver is the source of the 0.426% number; the simulation is a consistency check, not a replacement.
