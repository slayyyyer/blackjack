What changed

The 95% interval narrowed from 1.429 points at 100k to 0.451 at 1M and 0.143 at 10M. The observed edge moved 0.241% → 0.367% → 0.417%.

The first 100,000 and 1,000,000 rounds are not new simulations. They are the same nested sequences as the smaller reports.

What did not change

The rules, seed, cut card, chart and insurance threshold are identical. The exact fresh-shoe edge remains 0.426%. The 10M point estimate sits 0.008 points below it, well inside the interval.

Split EV still uses the documented independent-hand approximation. The chart is total-dependent, not composition-dependent.

What the correction changed

The earlier 0.518% result did not expose a model gap. Its insurance selector divided the post-hole ten count by the pre-hole unseen count, so a hidden ten made the shoe look poorer in tens than it was. At the 1/3 threshold that selected the wrong hands: 82,698 insurance bets won 26.9% of the time instead of the 34.9% their own densities implied.

Tracking visible cards instead moves the same 10M card sequence to 0.417%, with insurance turning from a 0.080-point cost into a 0.021-point gain for the player. The exact 0.426% reference is inside the 0.346%–0.489% interval: the simulation now agrees with the solver within error. A ±0.02-point interval would take roughly 127.3 million rounds.
