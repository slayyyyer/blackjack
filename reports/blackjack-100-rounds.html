What is exact

Dealer distributions and action EVs are exact for their composition. The 0.426% fresh-shoe edge is the composition-aware fixed-strategy solve; its split EV uses the infinite-deck approximation below.

The 100,000-hand reference uses the same play engine and policy. Its win/loss/push rates and 1.153-unit standard deviation are the long-run baseline used on the previous pages.

What is approximated

Split EV uses the documented independent-hand approximation. The chart is total-dependent, not composition-dependent. The insurance grade uses the unseen ten density with the hole card treated as one unseen card, not a peek.

The play engine settles the real shoe composition, but the coach’s EV is chart-based.

What this does not prove

One hundred rounds cannot distinguish a 0.426% edge from zero. The coach ledger is not the whole table: 85 graded rounds expected -1.21 and returned -6.00; the other 15 settled before a grade and returned +6.00. Those pieces reconcile to +0.00.
